# What the video shows

The file is `frisket.mp4` in this folder. It is a voice walkover on a screen recording of the installed Frisket app. The picture is the real app. The page is the local fixture in `fixture/`.

The recording is the built-in display only. The desktop icons were hidden. The pages in frame are the fixture. History was emptied before the take and only holds shots from that take.

In order:

- The Northline page is on screen. The price sits on the token, and the button is cut off.
- Command-Shift-4. The size is on the selection. The grid labeled “Pixels under the pointer” is on the headline.
- The editor. Solid redaction draws a black bar. Arrow is the active tool, and a small arrow sits on the price. Done returns a tighter thumbnail.
- Command-Shift-5. The thumbnail is the browser window.
- Command-Shift-6. The selection sits on the paragraph and the sections under it. Done keeps that shot.
- Copy Text on the scrolling thumbnail. The alert reads “Copied 600 characters.”
- Command-Shift-1. History shows those shots.
- The menu-bar menu lists the same keys.

The paste into TextEdit was recorded and then dropped. The open panel showed the account name in its sidebar, so that clip is not in the film and not in this folder.

The voice is the system voice Samantha, recorded separately and mixed in Remotion 4.0.527. There is no music.
